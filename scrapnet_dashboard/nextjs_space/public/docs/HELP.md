## Quick Start Guide

Getting started with Bedrock ESG takes just a few minutes. Follow these steps to begin your journey toward verified sustainability:

1. **Create Your Account**  
   Navigate to the registration page and enter your professional email and secure password (minimum 8 characters). Click **Create Account** to proceed.

2. **Complete Verification Onboarding**  
   After registration, complete the verification flow. Select your organizational role—**Agricultural Producer**, **Industrial Operator**, **Real Estate Developer**, or **Individual Contributor**—to configure your dashboard.

3. **Accept Compliance Agreements**  
   Review and accept the Terms of Service, Carbon Rights Transfer agreement, and Audit Monitoring consent. These ensure your contributions meet institutional standards.

4. **Connect Your Verification Wallet (Optional)**  
   For blockchain-attested records, connect your Solana wallet from the **Verification Vault**. This enables immutable audit proofs on-chain.

5. **Submit Your First Contribution**  
   Navigate to the appropriate verification module (e.g., **Biochar CDR Calculator** for producers) and enter your operational data. Click **Calculate** to see your verified impact estimate.

6. **Await Institutional Verification**  
   Your submission enters the verification queue. An authorized verification agent will review and certify your data, after which your contribution becomes officially attested.

## Core Capabilities

### **Industrial-Grade Carbon Verification**
The Biochar CDR Calculator provides institutional-grade verification of Carbon Dioxide Removal credits based on production data.

**How to use it:**
- Navigate to **Biochar** → **Calculator** from the sidebar
- Enter feedstock type, weight (kg), and pyrolysis temperature
- Click **Calculate CDR Credits** to receive your verified impact estimate
- Save the batch to submit for institutional verification

---

### **Verification Vault**
Your Verification Vault serves as your institutional dashboard, displaying all verified contributions and their economic value under the Universal Law (70/20/10 distribution).

**How to use it:**
- Access via **Vault** in the sidebar
- View your total verified balance, pending verifications, and attested contributions
- Select materials diverted or processed to calculate real-time institutional impact
- Generate blockchain attestations to create permanent, auditable records on Solana

---

### **Producer & Contribution Management**
For verification agents and institutional administrators, this capability enables registration of contributing producers and tracking of their verified environmental contributions.

**How to use it:**
- Navigate to **Producers** to view all registered contributors
- Click **Register Producer** to onboard a new contributor
- Track each producer's feedstock contributions and verification status
- Use **Credits** to issue or process verified contributions

---

### **Verification Agent Portal**
Verification agents play a critical role in validating environmental contributions. The verification queue displays all pending submissions awaiting institutional review.

**How to use it:**
- Access **Agent Portal** → **Verification Queue**
- Review each pending biochar batch or recovery log
- Click **Verify** to certify or provide detailed rejection rationale
- Verified items automatically trigger blockchain attestation

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### **Q: I cannot access my account. What should I do?**
**A:** First, verify you're using the correct email and password. If credentials are forgotten, use the "Reset Password" function on the login page. If your account shows "Verification Pending" status, institutional review may be in progress—contact support if this extends beyond 48 hours.

### **Q: How long does institutional verification take?**
**A:** Most submissions receive verification within 24-48 hours. High-volume periods may extend timelines. Monitor your submission status in the **Batches** or **Credits** section.

### **Q: Can I export verification data for compliance reporting?**
**A:** Yes. Navigate to **Biochar** → **Batches** and select **Export**. Individual batch certificates or bulk records can be downloaded as JSON files compatible with the Hedera Guardian Framework for institutional compliance.

### **Q: What is the 70/20/10 distribution I see in my vault?**
**A:** This is the **Universal Law** governing value distribution:  
- **70%** is allocated to the Contributor (Founder Yield)  
- **20%** supports Stewardship Initiatives (Debt-Erasure programs)  
- **10%** funds Public Resilience (community infrastructure)

### **Q: How do I connect my verification wallet?**
**A:** In the **Verification Vault**, select **Connect Wallet**. Your browser extension (Phantom, Solflare, etc.) will prompt for connection approval. Once confirmed, your wallet address links to your account for generating attestations.

### **Q: What if pyrolysis temperature is below 350°C?**
**A:** The system will flag a compliance warning. Biochar produced below 350°C may not meet stability requirements for institutional verification. The batch can be saved but may receive additional scrutiny during verification.

## Troubleshooting

| **If this occurs...** | **Resolution:** |
|-----------------------|----------------|
| Login fails with "Invalid credentials" | Verify email spelling and password accuracy. Use password reset if uncertain. |
| Dashboard displays no data | Ensure onboarding completion and role selection. Refresh the page after several seconds. |
| Calculation interface is unresponsive | Verify all required fields contain valid data. Check for error indicators below input fields. |
| Wallet connection unsuccessful | Ensure wallet extension (Phantom, Solflare) is installed and unlocked. Refresh and retry. |
| Export produces empty file | Confirm saved batches exist. Apply filters and verify at least one record is present. |
| Verification queue shows "0 items" despite submission | Submission may be processing. Allow 5 minutes and refresh. Confirm submission in **Batches**. |
| "Compliance required" notification appears | Navigate to **Settings** → **Compliance** and complete all required agreements. |
| Blockchain attestation fails | Ensure wallet contains sufficient SOL for transaction fees. Verify network connectivity and retry. |

## Glossary of Terms

- **CDR (Carbon Dioxide Removal):** The verified process of capturing CO₂ from the atmosphere and storing it long-term.
- **Biochar:** A carbon-stable material produced by processing organic matter (feedstock) in a controlled, low-oxygen environment.
- **Pyrolysis Temperature:** The processing temperature used during biochar production; higher temperatures typically produce more stable carbon.
- **Attestation:** A verified, immutable record of environmental contribution stored on a blockchain ledger.
- **Universal Law:** The 70/20/10 value distribution framework governing all settlements within Bedrock ESG.
- **Verification Agent:** An authorized professional responsible for reviewing and certifying environmental contributions.
